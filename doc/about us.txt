1. About Us 
Youth Wetland Research Network是首个为热衷于湿地保护、生态系统科学和环境创新的高中生打造的全球性研究平台。我们致力于保护和恢复湿地，以保护它们的环境价值以及它们为人们提供的服务。我们通过由青年研究者、导师和变革者组成的社群来实现我们的目标。
Youth Wetland Research Network is the first global research platform for high school students passionate about wetland conservation, ecosystem science, and environmental innovation. We are dedicated to protecting and restoring wetlands for their environmental value as well as for the services they provide to people. We work through our network of young researchers, mentors, and changemakers to achieve our goals. 

What makes us, us?
是什么造就了我们，我们？	
Our Vision
我们的愿景 
致力于构建一个由全球青年引领的世界，在那里，湿地因其美丽、所承载的生命及所提供的资源而得到珍视与滋养。
To build a world led by global youth, where wetlands are treasured and nurtured for their beauty, the life they support, and the resources they provide.
Our Mission
我们的使命
激励和动员全球青年，通过研究与创新行动，为人类与自然守护和修复湿地。
To inspire and mobilize global youth to safeguard and restore wetlands for people and nature through research and innovative action.

Our Core Values
我们的核心价值观
可及性：让复杂的湿地科学变得易于理解和参与
创新：利用人工智能和技术增强研究能力
协作：在学生、科学家和社区之间建立全球联系
影响力：确保每个项目都能促进对现实世界的理解和保护

Accessibility: Making complex wetland science approachable and engaging
Innovation: Leveraging AI and technology to enhance research capabilities
Collaboration: Building global connections between students, scientists, and communities	
Impact: Ensuring every project contributes to real-world understanding and protection

我们的故事
Youth Wetland Research Network 成立于2024年，旨在弥合学术湿地科学与高中教育之间的鸿沟。我们相信，有意义的环境研究不应等到大学才开始——全球的青少年都应获得工具、指导和平台，为全球湿地保护事业贡献力量。
Our Story:
Youth Wetland Research Network  was founded in 2024 to bridge the gap between academic wetland science and high school education. We believe that meaningful environmental research shouldn't wait until college—and that teenagers everywhere deserve the tools, mentorship, and platform to contribute to global conservation efforts.

创始人说 
The founder Says:

My name is Sun Luhan, born in 2008 on Chongming Island, Shanghai. Growing up amidst the breath and tides of the wetlands, I have personally witnessed their changes and the challenges they face. I often wondered: why must understanding and protecting this cradle of life wait until adulthood?
Wetlands will not wait, and action should begin in youth. 

In 2024, I founded the "Youth Wetland Research Network." We are committed to breaking down the barriers between academic research and secondary education, providing young people worldwide with professional tools, mentorship, and an international platform, empowering the younger generation to genuinely engage in the forefront of wetland research and conservation.

We believe that science should not just be knowledge in textbooks but a tool for everyone to change the world. Starting from Chongming, connecting the world—we are here, waiting for every one of you who loves nature and is willing to speak up for wetlands.

Youth Wetland Research Network—where research begins in youth, and conservation becomes a shared mission.
